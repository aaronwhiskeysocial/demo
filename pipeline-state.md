### NEW — `first-west-explorer-black-corn-2026`. VERIFIED. The best find in a week.

**Whisky Advocate, Aug 11 2026, "Whiskey of the Week: First West Explorer," Danny Brandon;
tasting note signed David Fleming.** Published yesterday, so it runs Aug 18 one week old.
This is the sourced-sensory item Aug 18 has been missing since it was staged.

What the WA page supports verbatim:
- **92 points · ABV 47.5% · SRP $50 · Availability: Nationwide as a permanent release.**
  Permanent and nationwide at $50 clears the forward bar without qualification.
- **WA's own tasting note** (its reviewer tasted it): nose ginger, dried herbs, generous cinnamon
  spice, caramel, baked apple, vanilla, barrel char; palate mellow and smooth with nutmeg, almond,
  vanilla coconut ice cream; finish warm and full, chocolate, spice, peanuts. "Well balanced and
  delicious." Attribute to Whisky Advocate.
- Blend of **two bourbons**: base is a wheated bourbon, 70% yellow dent corn / 18% wheat / 12%
  malted barley; the other is a ryed bourbon, 70% **Baby Black** corn / 18% rye / 12% malted barley.
- **Age-stated 5 years** on the back label, contains some older liquid including stock laid down
  seven years ago.
- Father-son **Rick and Ricky Johnson**; the grain interest comes from the family's high-end
  popcorn business. Baby Black is proprietary, developed exclusively for them, grown partly on
  their own farms and partly on contract in Kentucky and Indiana. Onyx kernels about a quarter the
  size of yellow, ears 5–6 inches.
- First West is a sister label to 15 Stars, debuted last August with three permanent bottlings.
- Black corn is rare elsewhere: Sierra Norte (Oaxaca), Don Michael Distillery (Lima, Peru).

**Two conflicts to handle before printing — both real, both resolved here:**
1. **"Contract-distilled" vs "in-house."** WA says Explorer is the brand's first expression to
   **entirely use contract-distilled liquid from Bardstown Bourbon Co.** The brand's own release
   is headlined **"the Brand's First With 100% In-House Distilled Bourbon."** These contradict on
   their face; the brand means distilled to its own spec from its own grains rather than bought as
   finished whiskey. **Print WA's version — it is the link-out, and it is the more accurate one.**
   Do not repeat "in-house distilled."
2. **Sensory provenance.** The brand release carries its own notes (cherry, banana pudding, vanilla
   wafers, meringue). **Never mix these with WA's.** Use WA's only. Separately, the line "the
   Johnsons say that Baby Black contributes butter, peppermint, and sweetness" is the **producers'**
   claim inside the WA piece — attribute to the Johnsons if used, not to WA.

**Second source:** the brand release, carried by Fred Minnick (May 27), Breaking Bourbon's press
release page and BevNET. Primary release + independent WA review clears the rule. Release confirms
**95 proof (= 47.5% ABV) and MSRP $49.99**, both consistent with WA. Do not link the release —
WA is the link-out.

**Collision:** WA's note contains "vanilla coconut ice cream," and **Macallan Harmony VI (coconut)
is staged for the same edition.** Not fatal — coconut is incidental in one and the whole story in
the other — but do not run both without noticing, and never let coconut appear as the hook twice.
