Logistics

Home base: Elizabethtown

Every day is a drive in and a drive out. The Louisville shuttle doesn't serve Elizabethtown, and rideshare out of Bardstown is notoriously unreliable on festival weekend — settle who's driving home before the day starts, not at the end of it.

Town-center to town-center, non-stop, no traffic or parking time included — distance-cities.com. Every event in this schedule carries a "from E-town" directions link for a live estimate.

Parking

No dedicated attendee lot at the festival. Plan on a nearby cash lot or street parking; the grounds are at 114 N 5th St and most downtown venues sit within the 3rd–5th Street / Stephen Foster Ave grid.

Tickets

Weekend passes sold out April 14. General Admission still turns up on the festival's TIXR resale marketplace, new this year. Speakeasy access is a separate per-day pass.

Festival hours

VIP entry 11am daily; General Admission noon. Distilleries on-site close 4pm; Vendor Marketplace 3pm; food trucks 2–4pm by vendor. Thursday's grounds-open hours weren't published — only the 6–9:30pm kickoff is scheduled.

Fri & Sat 12–6 PM · Sun 12–4 PM

Entry

RFID wristbands mailed Sept 3–7 (or will-call via your TIXR account); required for entry, exit, and premium-event access. Bring a valid ID. Re-entry allowed through the day.

Weather

Rain-or-shine. Rain is common and the grounds get muddy in moderate-to-heavy storms — the guide's advice is a spare pair of mud shoes. Expect low-to-mid 80s°F.

Bring

  • Empty refillable water bottle (hydration stations at both ends of the grounds)
  • Valid photo ID and your wristband
  • Individual folding lawn chair, if you want one
  • Spare shoes for mud
  • Clear bag (max 12″×6″×12″) or small clutch / fanny pack

Leave in the car

Outside alcohol, external glassware (KBF glassware only), food or beverages, large bags, coolers, scooters/skateboards/bicycles, drones, tents/canopies, wagons/carts, signs/banners, pets.

Companion animals with proper paperwork are allowed. All bags are subject to search.

Lockers

Rentable lockers on-site for bottle purchases and gear — reserve ahead via the festival site. Useful if you're not walking back to a hotel.

Louisville shuttle (for reference)

Runs between Tempo by Hilton in NuLu and the festival; tickets via TIXR. Not useful from Elizabethtown — listed in case anyone in the party is based in Louisville.
